Privacy Policy

How we protect and handle your health information

Introduction

MediPortal Connect ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal and health information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to safeguard it.

Information We Collect

  • Personal identification details (name, email, phone number, date of birth)
  • Appointment information and scheduling history
  • Laboratory test results and reports
  • AI chat interactions for support purposes
  • Notification preferences and settings

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and manage your patient portal account
  • To schedule, reschedule, and cancel appointments
  • To display laboratory results and related health information
  • To send appointment reminders and notifications
  • To improve our services and user experience

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (AES-256) and security practices to protect your data. Access to sensitive health information is restricted to authorized personnel only. All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted.

HIPAA Compliance

MediPortal Connect is designed with HIPAA compliance in mind. We follow strict privacy and security rules to ensure your health information remains confidential and secure.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only with healthcare providers involved in your care, or as required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@mediportal.care.
