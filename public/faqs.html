Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about MediPortal.

How can we help you?

Browse the topics below or contact our support team if you need further assistance.

Click the “Get Started” or “Create Account” button on the homepage. Fill in your full name, email address, phone number, and a secure password. After submitting, you’ll receive a verification email. Click the link in that email to activate your account.

Please check your spam or junk folder. If you still don’t see it, make sure you entered the correct email address. You can request a new verification email by signing in and following the prompt, or contact support for help.

Once logged in, go to the Appointments section and click “Book New Appointment”. You’ll be guided through selecting a department, doctor, date, and time. Review your details and confirm. You’ll receive a confirmation notification.

Yes. From your appointment list, select the appointment you want to change. Use the “Reschedule” button to pick a new date and time, or “Cancel” to cancel it. Please cancel at least 24 hours in advance to avoid any potential fees.

Navigate to the Lab Results section from your dashboard. You’ll see a list of your available reports. Click “View Details” on any report to see the full results, including test components and your doctor’s comments.

Yes. We use industry-standard AES-256 encryption for all data and follow HIPAA-compliance practices. Your information is only accessible to you and the healthcare providers involved in your care.

The AI Support Assistant provides emotional support and helps with logistical questions like booking appointments or understanding lab results. It does not provide medical advice. If you have clinical questions, the AI will redirect you to your doctor or care team.

Go to Settings and scroll to Notification Preferences. You can enable or disable email, SMS, appointment reminders, and lab result notifications. Click “Save Preferences” to apply changes.

Click “Forgot Password?” on the sign‑in page. Enter your registered email address and we’ll send you a password reset link. The link expires after 30 minutes for security.

Visit the Contact & Help page for phone and email support. You can also use the AI Support Assistant for quick guidance.
